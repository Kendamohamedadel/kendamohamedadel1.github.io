About Kenda

Kenda Mohamed Adel is a young Egyptian Taekwondo athlete who began her Taekwondo journey at the age of four.

She has achieved major success in both Kyorugi and Poomsae, including two Egyptian National Kyorugi titles, three Egyptian National Poomsae titles, and three Egypt Cup Poomsae titles.

Kenda proudly represents Skills Platinum Sport and continues to work toward her goal of representing Egypt internationally.

Coaches: Captain Khaled Sultan & Captain Mohamed Sultan
